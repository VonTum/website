Hi all! This is my personal webpage ^-^

I've probably been programming for longer than is healthy. I started out near the start of high school programming BASIC on my TI-84, in dasterdly competition and collaboration with my friend Matthias Vandersanden. After a while, unsatisfied with the slow speed of TI-BASIC, I started dabbling in z80 assembly, creating a super-fast [maze generator](https://www.cemetech.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=10658&start=0) (and an `AsmPrgrm76` calculator-bricking scourge at my school). Around the same time I started doing "real" programming in Java, in which I built a few [minecraft plugins](https://github.com/VonTum/ItemFinder) and [little experiments](https://github.com/VonTum/Fractal) for my school. After building a small [2D physics engine](https://github.com/VonTum/Physics2D-Java) in Java, I joined forces with Matthias once again to build our biggest project thus far: [Physics3D](https://github.com/ThePhysicsGuys/Physics3D), this time in C++. I believe we ended up working on the thing for almost 5 years! Sadly, as we both moved on to our final years of university, and had to focus on our respective master's theses, progress on Physics3D slowed and then halted. I moved on to the [Dedekind Project](https://github.com/VonTum/Dedekind), a project in which I calculated the 9th Dedekind Number. It involved designing a dedicated Hardware accelerator to speed up computing enough such that we could feasibly run it on the [Paderborn University Noctua 2](https://pc2.uni-paderborn.de/systems-and-services/noctua-2) FPGA cluster. After 3 years of work and half a year of runtime, we found it! After this project, I joined PC2 to work on my new research project: Designing a new Hardware Design Language called [SUS](https://github.com/pc2/sus-compiler) to fix the sorry state of hardware design, and make it easier to design hardware accelerators, like the one that computed the 9th Dedekind Number.
