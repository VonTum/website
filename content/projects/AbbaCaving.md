One of two Minecraft server plugins I ever wrote. The Second one was [ItemFinder](/projects/ItemFinder). 

Abba Caving is a minigame where all players are randomly teleported to a cave system underground. They each have 15 minutes to dig up as many (and as valuable) as possible ores. After 15 minutes, points are assigned to all ores mined, and the player with the most points takes home everyone's takings. 

I wrote this plugin because on the Cemetech server we were doing this minigame, and having it supported more directly was nice.
