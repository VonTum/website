Around my first or second year of university, I and a group of friends got together and created a student organization for gaming at KU Leuven. We had bought a second-hand server, and were hosting Minecraft, Factorio, and other servers there. The response from our fellow students was overwhelming! I would often look at the website while attending lectures, and I'd see dozens of people online. Our [Discord](https://discord.gg/8Pd5Stuy3N) community balooned to more than 200 members, and the staff team expanded to 11. Uniluc operated over some 4 years, with the main Minecraft server seeing 4 full iterations. Time and time again people filled our world with giant beautiful builds; truly spectacular when viewed on the world map. 

You can view the last version of the website on the [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/web/20240920152232/http://uniluc.com/)

If I ever bother to reinstate it, the domain is [uniluc.com](uniluc.com)
