Around my first or second year of university, I and a group of friends got together and created a student organization for gaming at KU Leuven. We had bought a second-hand server, and were hosting Minecraft, Factorio, and other servers there. We also have (had) a [Discord](https://discord.gg/8Pd5Stuy3N). Though since all of us have now graduated, and we didn't manage to hand it over to a next generation, the project faltered and eventually died. Though it was a lovely ride while it lasted. 

If I ever bother to reinstate it, the domain is [uniluc.com](uniluc.com)
