What is SteamCrafted?

SteamCrafted is a SkyBlock survival server that adds unique ways of completing challenges and collecting new items. We try to make skyblock as smooth as it can be.

No pesky commands you can’t remember
Forget about the maze of signs in spawn
Get a reward when you complete a challenge!

 
Once you joined the server “steamcrafted.net” you will be given a Compass. This is going to be your new best friend. Right click with it to get started!
 
You can use the Compass to:
  • Create your island/ Get back to your island
  • Check and complete your challenges (You will lose the required items and get a reward in return!)
  • Check back on the commands you need to invite a friend, restart your island, …
 
That is everything you need to know for now. Have fun playing!
